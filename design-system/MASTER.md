## Typography
--font-jakarta: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif;
--font-space-grotesk: 'Space Grotesk', system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif;
--font-jetbrains-mono: 'JetBrains Mono', monospace;
--font-kalam: 'Kalam', cursive;

## Usage
- Headings: Space Grotesk
- Body: Plus Jakarta Sans
- Code: JetBrains Mono
- Accent/Handwriting: Kalam

## Effects
- Subtle micro-interactions (150-300ms)
- Chalkboard texture background
- Sticky note variations
- Glass-like effects with dashed borders
- Smooth scrollbar

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Using emojis as icons
- Inconsistent icon styles
- Low contrast text
- Layout-shifting interactions
- Inconsistent spacing
